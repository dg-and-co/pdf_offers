Klippi
Overview · What the platform is

Klippi is a loyalty platform for restaurants, cafés and other stores. Customers keep digital cards in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet, so they never lose their stamping progress. Every card a customer claims also gives the store the customer's name and phone number, which the store uses for SMS marketing.

Roles

Three people touch the system

CustomerHolds cards in the wallet

Claims a card by scanning a QR code and entering name and phone number. Shows the card's QR code at the counter to get stamps or pay with a balance.

CashierUses the Tikki app

The home screen has two buttons: Scan and Create card. Scan opens a card's profile to add stamps or deduct an amount. Create card issues a Gjafakort or Inneignarkort.

ManagerRuns the Klippi backend

Designs the stamp card, creates the stand with the QR code for customers, sees every card ever created, and uses collected phone numbers for SMS marketing.

Card types

Three kinds of cards a store can create

01Stamp card

Stamps on purchases. A full card earns something free or a discount. Claimed from the stand at the counter.

02Gjafakort (gift card)

A prepaid amount. Delivered on screen, by email, or as a physical card with a reference code. Redeemed by deducting from the balance.

03Inneignarkort (store credit)

Credit issued when an item is returned, matching the printed inneignarnóta. Redeemed like a gift card.

Overview · Building blocks
Building blocks

What the platform is made of

Klippi backend
Web app for the manager. Card design, stand and QR creation, list of all cards, customer database.
Tikki app
Cashier's phone app. Two actions: Scan a card, or Create a card.
Wallet cards
Apple Wallet and Google Wallet passes, each with its own QR code. They update automatically when stamps or balances change.
Receiving flow
The web page a customer lands on after scanning. Asks for name and phone number, then releases the card into the wallet.
Claim pages
A store subpage such as giftcard.mettasport.is, which Klippi sets up for each client, or klippi.is/giftcard. Used to claim a physical gift card with full name, phone number and reference code.
Customer database
Name and phone number (and email when used) saved on every claim. Used for SMS about new offers, drops and limited deals.
Tikki home screenScanCreate card
Rule that applies to every card type: a card is only released after the customer enters name and phone number. Claiming a card and giving the store a marketing contact are the same action.

Flow 01 · Stamp card
Flow 01

Stamp card

The core loyalty loop. The manager designs the card once, customers claim it from a stand at the counter, and the cashier adds stamps by scanning the card.

  1. 01
    ManagerDesign the card and create the stand

    In the Klippi backend the manager designs the stamp card, then creates a stand with a QR code that customers can scan at the counter.

  2. 02
    CustomerClaim the card into the wallet

    The customer scans the stand's QR code and enters the receiving flow: name and phone number. The card is then released into Apple Wallet or Google Wallet, and the phone number is saved to the store's database.

  3. 03
    CashierAdd a stamp on purchase

    The cashier opens Tikki, taps Scan and scans the QR code on the customer's card. The card profile opens and the cashier adds stamps to that customer.

  4. 04
    SystemCard updates automatically

    The card in the customer's wallet updates on its own with the new stamp count.

  5. 05
    CustomerEarn the reward

    When the card is full the customer gets something free or at a discount.

  6. 06
    ManagerSMS marketing

    The store uses the phone numbers collected through claims to send SMS about new offers, drops and limited deals.

Flow 02 · Gjafakort
Flow 02

Gjafakort (gift card)

Payment happens on the store's own terminal. Klippi creates the card, delivers it in one of three ways, and later deducts purchases from its balance.

A · Create
  1. 01
    Customer · CashierPay for the gift card

    The customer asks for a gift card. The cashier asks for the amount, enters it into the payment terminal, and the customer pays.

  2. 02
    CashierCreate the card in Tikki

    Create card → Gjafakort → enter the amount → Create. A unique card is created and is always visible in the Klippi backend.

  3. 03
    CashierChoose how to deliver it

    The cashier asks whether to display the card now, send it by email, or produce a physical card. Three options, described below.

B · Deliver
Option 1Display now

A QR code fills the cashier's screen. The customer scans it, enters name and phone number in the receiving flow, and the card lands in their wallet. The customer's details are saved.

Option 2Send via email

The cashier types the customer's email address in the app and sends the card.

Option 3Reference code (physical gift)

A 5-character code of letters and numbers appears. The cashier takes a pre-printed physical gift card from the cabinet, fills in the two blanks (amount and the 5-box code), and packs it in an envelope. Claim instructions are printed at the bottom of the card.

Flow 02 · Gjafakort, continued
Flow 02 · continued

Gjafakort: claim and redeem

A physical gift card is claimed by the person who receives it. Any gift card, however it was delivered, is redeemed the same way at the counter.

C · Claim a physical card
  1. 01
    RecipientOpen the envelope

    The card shows the amount, the 5-character reference code and, at the bottom, instructions for claiming it.

  2. 02
    RecipientGo to the claim page

    Either the store's own claim page, for example giftcard.mettasport.is, which Klippi sets up for each client, or klippi.is/giftcard, which works for every store because every reference code is unique.

  3. 03
    Recipient · SystemFill in three fields

    Full name, phone number, reference code. The card is released to the wallet and the recipient's details are saved for marketing.

D · Redeem
  1. 01
    CashierTotal the purchase

    Scan the items and note the total amount.

  2. 02
    CashierScan the gift card

    Tikki → Scan → scan the QR code on the customer's gift card. The gift card profile appears.

  3. 03
    Cashier · SystemDeduct and confirm

    Deduct the purchase amount from the balance and tap Confirm. The card in the wallet updates automatically.

Flow 03 · Inneignarkort
Flow 03

Inneignarkort (store credit)

For stores with a store-credit policy on returns. The credit follows the printed inneignarnóta and is redeemed like a gift card.

  1. 01
    Customer · CashierReturn the item

    The customer returns an item. The cashier scans it, sees the amount it cost, and prints the inneignarnóta.

  2. 02
    CashierCreate the credit card

    Tikki → Create card → Inneignarkort → enter the amount from the inneignarnóta.

  3. 03
    CashierDeliver

    Display now (customer scans the QR code and claims it) or Send via email.

  4. 04
    Cashier · SystemRedeem on the next visit

    Scan the Inneignarkort QR code, edit the balance in the card profile, confirm. The card updates automatically.

Build summary · Screens, data and rules
Build summary

What has to exist

Everything above, condensed into the screens, data, lifecycle and rules an implementation needs to cover.

Tikki app screens
Home (Scan, Create card) · Scan → Card profile with add stamps, deduct amount, Confirm · Create card → Gjafakort or Inneignarkort → amount → Create → Display now, Send via email, Reference code (Gjafakort only).
Web screens
Manager backend (design card, create stand and QR, list of all cards, customers) · Receiving flow (name, phone → release card to wallet) · Claim page (full name, phone, reference code), per store or at klippi.is/giftcard.
Data
Store · Manager · Cashier · Card template (design) · Card (type: stamp, gift or credit; stamp count or balance; unique reference code; QR; status) · Customer (name, phone, email) · History of stamps and deductions.
Card lifecycle
Created → delivered (display now, email, or reference code) → claimed with name and phone → in the wallet → updated on each scan (stamp added or amount deducted) → full or empty.
Integrations
Apple Wallet and Google Wallet passes with automatic updates · QR code on every card · SMS sending · Email delivery. Payment stays on the store's terminal, outside Klippi.
Rules

Things that are always true

  • A card is released only after name and phone number are entered
  • Every card has its own unique reference code, across all stores
  • Reference codes are 5 characters, letters and numbers
  • Wallet cards update automatically after every change
  • Every created card is visible in the Klippi backend
  • Reference code delivery exists for Gjafakort only
